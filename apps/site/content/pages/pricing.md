## What "masked" means {#masked}

On Free, every alert is real — the leak, the score, the plan and the money at stake — but the person is shown as a masked tag instead of a name. Masking happens on Dripmetric's servers, so a free project's pages and API responses never contain an email, name, user id or trait. Starter reveals the person behind the tag.
