Dripmetric is a revenue-leak detector for self-serve SaaS, so it is narrower than the tools it is most often compared with. Product analytics platforms such as PostHog, Mixpanel and Amplitude let you explore any event data and build the funnels, queries and alerts you need. Dripmetric watches for revenue leaks and buying intent over product and payment events together, ready on day one. Many teams use both.

Each comparison is dated, and its facts about the other tool come from that tool's own documentation. Products change, so check them before you decide.
