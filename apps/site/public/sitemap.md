# Dripmetric content map

Inventory scope: complete. Public documents: 10.

Dripmetric is a revenue-leak detector for self-serve SaaS. It reads product usage and, optionally, payment events together, watches for nine kinds of revenue leak (such as onboarding stalls, trials that end unpaid and likely buyers who go quiet), scores how close each user is to buying, and sends alerts to Slack or Discord plus a weekly leak report. It needs no billing integration. It is in private beta, free during the beta, with a waitlist.
