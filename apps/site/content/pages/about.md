**Dripmetric is a revenue-leak detector for self-serve SaaS.** It shows where and how much revenue leaks between a visitor's first visit and their first payment, from the product and payment events a team already sends. It needs no billing integration.

## What Dripmetric does {#what}

- **Finds revenue leaks.** It watches for nine kinds of leak, from a signup who stalls in onboarding to a payment that fails. See [the nine revenue leaks](https://dripmetric.com/leaks).
- **Shows who is closest to buying.** Every tracked user gets a buying-intent score from 0 to 100. See [what a buying-intent (PQL) score is](https://dripmetric.com/buying-intent).
- **Tells you in time to act.** An alert in Slack, Discord or the app when a leak is found, and a leak report every Monday.
- **Puts a number on each leak.** Valued at your own prices, with the working shown beside the number.

## What Dripmetric is not {#not}

- **Not an email or drip-campaign tool.** It does not send email, drip campaigns or in-app messages to your users. The only messages it sends go to you.
- **Not a billing integration.** It never connects to Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy or any other billing provider. Payment events arrive only because you send them.
- **Not a general product analytics platform.** It has no session replay, feature flags, experiments or surveys. See [how Dripmetric compares](https://dripmetric.com/compare).

## Who it is for {#who}

Small self-serve SaaS teams, from a few hundred signups a month, who want to know where people stop between signing up and paying. It suits products where people sign up and pay without talking to sales.

## Where it stands today {#status}
