A **buying-intent score** shows how close each person is to paying, judged from what they do in your product: reaching the point where it works for them, hitting a limit, reading your pricing, starting checkout. It is a product-qualified-lead (PQL) score for self-serve software, where most people buy without ever talking to sales. Dripmetric gives every tracked user one.

## Why it matters {#why}

Most people who sign up never pay, so the few who are close to buying are easy to miss in the crowd.

- Most trials end without a payment: the median free-to-paid conversion rate across 200 software products was 8%. — [Kyle Poyar with ChartMogul and ProductLed, The SaaS Conversion Report, February 2026](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/)
- Most new users never reach the point where the product works for them: the average activation rate across 62 B2B SaaS companies was 37.5%. — [Userpilot, SaaS Product Metrics Benchmark Report 2025](https://userpilot.com/saas-product-metrics/)

Third-party research, not Dripmetric data.

## What Dripmetric uses it for {#uses}

- **Who to look at first.** The people closest to buying are the ones a personal note is most likely to help.
- **An alert when a likely buyer goes quiet.** That is the [high-intent user went quiet](https://dripmetric.com/leaks#high-intent-quiet) leak.
- **The Monday report** lists the people who looked ready to buy and didn't.

## How it stays honest {#principles}

- **No black box.** The score is built from clear rules, not machine learning, and every point can be traced to something the person did.
- **It fades when people go quiet.** A score says how ready someone is now, not how ready they once were.
- **Changes are suggested, never automatic.** Once your product has enough paying customers, Dripmetric suggests adjustments; you decide whether to apply them.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### What is a product-qualified lead (PQL)?

A product-qualified lead is someone whose behaviour inside the product — activating, hitting a limit, inviting a teammate, reading pricing — shows they are likely to buy. Dripmetric's buying-intent score is a PQL score for self-serve software.

### Does the score use machine learning?

No. It is built from fixed rules. Once there is enough data, Dripmetric suggests adjustments for your product, and you decide whether to apply them.

### Can I see why someone scored what they did?

Yes. Every point comes with the behaviour that earned it. Alerts show a person as a masked tag by default.
