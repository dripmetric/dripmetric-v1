## What is included {#included}

**Free** — where and how much revenue leaks:

- Full funnel and step timing
- Buying-intent score
- All nine leaks, masked
- Instant masked alerts to Slack or Discord, plus an in-app feed
- Weekly leak report
- Tracking health and before-and-after proof
- Segment splits
- 90 days of raw events; daily summaries kept forever

**Starter** — everything in Free, plus who:

- The name, email and id behind every masked tag
- The signals behind each score
- 13 months of raw events

## What "masked" means {#masked}

On Free, every alert is real — the leak, the score, the plan and the money at stake — but the person is shown as a masked tag instead of a name. Masking happens on Dripmetric's servers, so a free project's pages and API responses never contain an email, name, user id or trait. Starter reveals the person behind the tag.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### How much does Dripmetric cost?

Dripmetric is free forever for where and how much revenue leaks. Starter, which shows who, is $24.99 a month. There is no yearly plan yet, and no charge for the free plan at any point.

### Is there a free plan?

Yes, with no time limit: the full funnel and step timing, the buying-intent score, all nine leaks (masked), instant masked alerts, the weekly leak report and 90 days of raw events.

### What is the difference between Free and Starter?

Free shows where and how much revenue leaks, with each person masked. Starter shows who — the name, email and id behind every masked tag — and the signals behind each buying-intent score, and keeps 13 months of raw events instead of 90 days.

### Is there a yearly plan?

Not yet. Starter is billed monthly at $24.99. A yearly price is not set, so there is nothing to quote; when there is one it will be on this page.
