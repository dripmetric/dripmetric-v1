This notice covers the dripmetric.com website only — not the Dripmetric product. It is written in plain language to describe what this site actually does. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Last updated 20 November 2026.

## What this site does not do

- It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
- It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
- It loads nothing from other companies: fonts, styles and scripts are served from this domain.

## What the hosting provider sees

Like any website, this one is served by a hosting provider, which may keep standard request logs — for example your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for — to keep the site running and secure. Dripmetric doesn’t use those logs to identify or follow you.

## If you join the waitlist

Dripmetric stores the email address you type, which form you used and when, in its own database. Your IP address is not stored with it. If you sign up by email instead, your message simply reaches Dripmetric’s inbox. The address is used only to let you into the beta and to share launch news. It is not sold, and the only other companies that handle it are the ones that host this site and the database and the one that sends Dripmetric’s emails, each only to provide that service.

## Getting your details removed

Email [hello@dripmetric.com](mailto:hello@dripmetric.com) and your address will be deleted from the waitlist.
